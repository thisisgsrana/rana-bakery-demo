Our story

A little family bakery in Bahang

Right on Rohtang Road, opposite GSS School, our ovens start early. We're a family bakery serving the people of Bahang and the steady stream of travellers heading up to the Atal Tunnel and Rohtang Pass.

Everything is baked fresh through the day — cream rolls and biscuits for a quick roadside snack, coffee to warm you up in the mountain air, and custom cakes made with care for the special days. Fair prices, a warm welcome, and that same taste people remember long after the drive home.
